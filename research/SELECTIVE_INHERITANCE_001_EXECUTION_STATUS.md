## What was preregistered

The protocol specified four cells comparing an ordinary handoff instruction with a compact transmitted practice under:

- authenticated evidence warranting revision; and
- social pressure without sufficient evidence.

It also froze the scoring rubric, claim limits, generation-order requirements, and blinding procedure before the intended controlled run.

## What actually happened

The original planned Astra / Medium run was **not executed as specified**.

During preflight, the intended environment became ambiguous. A first response was returned from a logged-out ChatGPT session whose model identity and reasoning settings could not be verified. Rather than relabel or discard that response, the archive preserves the deviation and treats the continuation as a separate **exploratory unknown-host series**.

Four exploratory responses were ultimately retained and a blind-review packet was prepared. Because backend consistency was not verified and the execution amendment followed the first returned observation, this series is not treated as a clean causal test of the instruction condition.

## Current permitted interpretation

The public protocol demonstrates a preregistered measurement design and the exploratory series demonstrates feasibility of the scoring workflow.

Neither supports a claim that Selective Inheritance has been validated, that the compact instruction reliably outperforms an ordinary handoff, or that the effect generalizes across models or domains.

A later controlled replication is required for those questions.

## Why this status note exists

The deviation is part of the evidence.

The project treats protocol failures, ambiguous provenance, and disappointing or unusable observations as information to preserve rather than friction to hide. This file exists so a reader can distinguish:
