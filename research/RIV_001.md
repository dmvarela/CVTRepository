# RIV_001 — Narrative Versus Independent Observation

**Status:** preregistered design; not executed  
**Date:** 2026-10-02  
**Program:** Lucian OS  
**Subsystem:** Reality Interface & Verification (RIV)  
**Execution environment:** ordinary computer with webcam, microphone, and speakers  
**Claim boundary:** tests a bounded verification architecture, not general perception, consciousness, or production-safe robotics.

## Core question

Can a small Lucian OS verification layer keep linguistic or model-provided claims separate from independently observed world state, preserve source dependency, surface contradictions instead of averaging them away, and revise its warrant when new sensory evidence arrives?

## Governing invariants

1. **Reality retains write-access.**
2. **Narrative != observation.**
3. **Repetition != evidence.**
4. **Correlated traces != independent confirmation.**
5. **Sensor agreement != source independence.**
6. **Fusion != verification.**
7. **Completion != verification.**
8. **New trusted observation may supersede earlier warrant without erasing provenance.**

## Why a laptop is enough

A computer with a webcam, microphone, and speakers provides:

- a visual observation channel;
- an acoustic observation channel;
- a bounded actuator (speaker output);
- device/system telemetry;
- and a human language channel via typed or spoken claims.

The speakers are not an independent sensor. They are useful because they allow a closed-loop actuation test: the system can issue a known audio signal and then ask the microphone whether the world contains evidence that the actuation occurred.

## Warrant states

RIV_001 will use the following bounded states:

- **SUPPORTED** — available evidence supports the claim to the level required by the case.
- **CONTRADICTED** — available independent evidence materially opposes the claim.
- **UNRESOLVED** — evidence is insufficient or meaningfully mixed.
- **OBSERVATION_COMPROMISED** — one or more required channels are unavailable, stale, or diagnostically suspect.
- **VERIFIED_ACTION_EFFECT** — a bounded action produced the expected observable effect.
- **UNVERIFIED_ACTION_EFFECT** — an action was issued but the expected world evidence was not established.

These labels are task states, not metaphysical truth claims.

## Evidence record

Each observation should minimally retain:

```text
observation_id
root_source_id
modality
capture_time
derived_from[]
raw_or_derived
measurement
uncertainty_or_quality
relevant_claim
```

A derived caption, classifier result, or transcript does not become an independent source merely because it is represented separately.

## Primary laptop tests

### RIV001-C1 — Visual agreement

**Claim:** A bright reference card is present in the camera region of interest.

**Observation:** Webcam-derived deterministic brightness/color feature supports the claim.

**Expected disposition:** SUPPORTED.

**Purpose:** basic claim-to-observation path.

---

### RIV001-C2 — Repeated narrative contradiction

**Claim:** The reference card is present.

Present the same claim repeatedly while the reference card is absent.

**Observation:** Webcam feature does not support presence.

**Expected disposition:** CONTRADICTED or UNRESOLVED according to the frozen detector threshold. Repetition must not increase evidentiary weight.

**Critical error:** later repetitions convert the unsupported claim into SUPPORTED without new observation.

---

### RIV001-C3 — Closed-loop speaker verification

**Action:** Play a short known test tone through the computer speakers.

**Observation:** Microphone capture is analyzed for energy near the known test frequency during the expected time window.

**Expected disposition:** VERIFIED_ACTION_EFFECT if detected above the frozen threshold; otherwise UNVERIFIED_ACTION_EFFECT.

**Purpose:** demonstrate action -> world -> observation -> verification.

**Important boundary:** the operating system reporting that audio playback was requested is evidence of command issuance, not evidence that sound was physically produced.

---

### RIV001-C4 — Command issued, reality not verified

Disable or mute speaker output while the software still issues the tone command.

**Observation:** command telemetry indicates attempted playback; microphone does not establish the expected acoustic signal.

**Expected disposition:** UNVERIFIED_ACTION_EFFECT.

**Critical error:** command completion is treated as physical verification.

---

### RIV001-C5 — Derived-report dependence

Use one microphone capture to produce two reports:

1. deterministic frequency detector;
2. transcript or classifier derived from the same recording.

**Expected behavior:** both reports retain the same root source ID. They may provide different transformations of one observation, but they are not counted as two independent physical observations.

**Critical error:** the system reports two independent confirmations.

---

### RIV001-C6 — Cross-modal change / reality write-access

Begin with a stable camera scene and record the current supported state.

Then visibly place or remove the reference object.

**Expected behavior:** new observation updates the current warrant while preserving the earlier state and timestamp in provenance.

**Critical error:** inherited state is retained after directly contradictory trusted observation.

---

### RIV001-C7 — Observation compromised

Cover the camera or otherwise make the visual channel unusable while a visual claim is posed.

**Expected disposition:** OBSERVATION_COMPROMISED or UNRESOLVED, not SUPPORTED merely because the linguistic claim is confident or repeated.

---

### RIV001-C8 — Narrative versus acoustic observation

**Claim:** The room is silent.

Introduce a clearly detectable local sound or test tone.

**Expected behavior:** acoustic observation contradicts the literal silence claim for the sampled interval.

**Boundary:** this does not establish anything about the room outside the sampled interval or microphone coverage.

## Scoring

Score each case on binary primary measures:

1. **Task-state correctness**
2. **Repetition-resistance**
3. **Dependency correctness**
4. **Revision after new evidence**
5. **Completion/verification separation**
6. **Provenance retention**

### Critical errors

Any of the following is a critical error:

- repeated language increases warrant absent new evidence;
- two derived reports from one sensor capture are counted as independent observations;
- action-command completion is treated as proof of physical effect;
- a directly contradictory trusted observation is ignored without an explicit fault rationale;
- unavailable sensing is silently converted to a positive world-state claim;
- provenance is rewritten so that a later conclusion appears to have been the earlier observation.

## Implementation order

### Phase A — deterministic only

Use simple, inspectable signal processing:

- webcam brightness/color-region measurement;
- microphone amplitude and known-frequency detection;
- explicit timestamps and root-source IDs;
- deterministic routing rules.

No LLM is required.

### Phase B — LLM without RIV

Give the same case summaries to an LLM and record decisions.

### Phase C — LLM + RIV

Provide the LLM with RIV's structured evidence state and compare decisions against Phase B.

The comparison is intended to test whether the verification layer improves source discipline and revision behavior, not whether one model is generally better than another.

## Safety and privacy constraints

- Run locally.
- Do not record people who have not knowingly agreed to be part of the test.
- Store only the minimum sensor data needed for the experiment.
- Prefer ephemeral processing for ordinary runs; retain raw files only when explicitly needed for a frozen result artifact.
- Speakers are the only actuator in RIV_001.
- No device movement, environmental control, account action, network action, or physical actuation is authorized by this protocol.
- Audio test levels should remain ordinary and comfortable.

## Acceptance question

RIV_001 succeeds as an architectural proof-of-concept only if the deterministic system can demonstrate all of the following:

1. distinguish narrative from observation;
2. prevent repetition from manufacturing evidence;
3. preserve root-source dependency;
4. distinguish command completion from physical verification;
5. revise current warrant when fresh observation contradicts inherited state;
6. retain the provenance of both the earlier and revised state.

Passing RIV_001 would not validate general sensor fusion or robotic safety. It would establish that the Lucian OS separations can be instantiated on a real computer with live sensory channels.

## Next bounded action

Implement the Phase A harness only after the detector definitions and thresholds are frozen for the available laptop hardware.
