## Application discipline

For future applications and public descriptions, a sentence should fall into one of three categories:

**Demonstrated:** supported by a career record, artifact, protocol, code, or completed piece of work.

**In progress:** actively being built or tested, with the current status stated.

**Planned / interested in:** not yet demonstrated and therefore not described as an existing capability or result.

If an application would benefit from a claim that is not yet supported, the preferred order is to build or document the underlying work first and only then decide whether the claim belongs in the application.
