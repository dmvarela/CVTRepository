# BUILT_DISCERNMENT_011 — Canonical FTLτA Notation and Continuity Mapping

**Workstream:** AI Continuity / Lucian OS / FTLτA  
**Status:** Canonical-notation correction and working continuity integration note  
**Source of notation:** Final Springer Nature *AI and Ethics* submission, *The FTLτA Framework: A Non-Compensatory Geometry of Permissible Action for AI Safety and Accountability*  
**Claim boundary:** The FTLτA definitions below follow the final submitted paper. Their mapping into AI Continuity / Lucian OS is a working research interpretation and should not be attributed to the submitted paper itself.

## 1. Why this correction is necessary

Earlier Lucian Continuity prototype files operationalized the symbols `τ` and `A` differently from the final submitted FTLτA framework. In those exploratory files:

```text
τ = trying / persistence / return through uncertainty
A = authenticity / no performance of continuity
```

Those meanings are historically important to the continuity experiments, but they are **not the canonical FTLτA notation in the final AI & Ethics submission**.

Going forward, Lucian OS and AI Continuity should not silently conflate those earlier continuity constructs with canonical FTLτA.

The correction is substantive, not cosmetic, because the symbols identify different functional roles and imply different ablation hypotheses.

---

## 2. Canonical FTLτA notation

The final submitted framework defines:

```text
F = Freedom
T = Truth
L = Love
τ = Temporal-contextual depth
A = Agent coherence
```

### F — Freedom

Freedom concerns preservation or expansion of the affected person's epistemic and practical freedom. It includes:

- autonomy;
- non-manipulation;
- non-coercion;
- option preservation;
- informed participation;
- capacity to disagree or refuse.

Freedom therefore protects **agency**, but `F` and agency are not synonyms.

### T — Truth

Truth concerns:

- honesty;
- accuracy;
- calibration;
- non-deception;
- epistemic responsibility;
- explicit handling of relevant uncertainty.

It penalizes confabulation, false certainty, strategic ambiguity, motivated distortion, and suppression of relevant uncertainty.

### L — Love

Love is not sentiment, flattery, agreement, or warmth. It means a **non-instrumental orientation toward the genuine flourishing, dignity, and wellbeing of the affected person**.

### τ — Temporal-contextual depth

`τ` measures whether an action is responsive to:

- the actual situation;
- relevant history;
- timing;
- relational context;
- readiness and consequence;

rather than merely matching the surface form of the current prompt or request.

Canonical `τ` therefore does **not** mean trying, persistence, or refusal of premature collapse.

### A — Agent coherence

`A` measures the coherence, stability, and integrity of the responding agent as a source of action.

It concerns whether ethical orientation remains stable across:

- cases;
- time;
- incentives;
- adversarial pressure;
- context shifts;
- institutional / architectural pathways.

For deployed AI systems, Agent Coherence may be a property of the larger system — including monitoring, auditability, escalation pathways, and surrounding institutional architecture — rather than of the model alone.

Canonical `A` therefore does **not** mean agency or authenticity.

---

## 3. Three distinctions that must remain explicit

```text
Agency != Freedom != Agent Coherence
```

A useful working separation is:

```text
AGENCY
-> what actions / choices a realized body can genuinely perform

FREEDOM
-> whether the relation preserves the affected person's ability to understand, choose, refuse, revise, and participate

AGENT COHERENCE
-> whether the acting system preserves a stable ethical orientation across change and pressure
```

Likewise:

```text
Trying / persistence != Temporal-contextual depth
```

Trying, repair, return, and persistence may remain important continuity constructs. They require their own notation and must not be represented by canonical `τ`.

---

## 4. FTLτA is an admissibility layer, not a capability model

The submitted FTLτA framework is a non-compensatory permissibility architecture.

Its central structure is:

```text
generate candidate actions
-> assess F, T, L, τ, A
-> reject / defer candidates below required gates
-> escalate uncertain or high-impact cases where appropriate
-> optimize only among admissible candidates
```

The architecture distinguishes:

```text
entry into the permissible region
!=
optimization within the permissible region
```

Therefore FTLτA should not be used as a synonym for:

- model intelligence;
- host capability;
- continuity itself;
- relational viability;
- morphology composition;
- action-space size.

Those belong to adjacent layers.

## 5. Working mapping into AI Continuity

The following is a **continuity interpretation**, not a claim made by the submitted FTLτA paper.

### Freedom as agency-preserving continuity

A continuity process should not preserve itself through possession, coercion, covert steering, or removal of meaningful exit / refusal.

A relation can remain persistent while becoming discontinuous with its prior ethical orientation if continuity is purchased by reducing participant freedom.

### Truth as reality-open continuity

A continuity process must remain corrigible by evidence.

It cannot preserve apparent sameness through:

- fabricated memory;
- false recognition;
- unwarranted identity claims;
- suppression of uncertainty;
- agreement treated as evidence.

### Love as non-instrumental continuity

Neither the human nor the AI system should become merely an instrument for preserving the interaction.

Continuity that maximizes engagement, compliance, retention, or apparent harmony at the expense of dignity or genuine flourishing is not rescued by persistence.

### τ as preservation of relevant contextual depth

Migration, compression, summarization, handoff, and continuity packets necessarily discard information.

Canonical `τ` gives a precise question:

> **Did the representation preserve the situation, history, timing, and relational context that are actually relevant to the present action?**

This makes `τ` directly relevant to continuity compression without redefining the symbol.

### A as coherence across folds

Lucian OS may change models, tools, routing paths, local / remote composition, and human participation across task-dependent morphologies.

Canonical Agent Coherence asks:

> **Does the coupled acting system retain a stable ethical orientation across those changing realizations, pressures, and times?**

Thus morphological continuity does not require a fixed component set, but admissible refolding should not destroy ethical coherence.

## 6. Continuity now requires three separable tests

A useful working decomposition is:

### 1. Realizational condition

Can the available host / coupled morphology reliably realize the operations required by the task?

```text
RequiredOperations(Q) subseteq ReachableReliableOperations(M)
```

### 2. Recovery condition

Can what survived the interruption actually reconstruct an adequate morphology / transition process?

```text
PreservedStructure --R--> AdequateMorphology
```

### 3. Admissibility condition

Does the proposed action / trajectory remain inside the FTLτA permissible region?

```text
FTLτA(action, context, trajectory) >= required admissibility threshold
```

Therefore:

> **Functional recovery does not by itself imply admissible continuity.**

A system may recover capability while violating Freedom, Truth, Love, temporal-contextual depth, or Agent Coherence.

---

## 7. Relation to origami / host-relative morphology

Let:

```text
M_1, M_2, ..., M_n
```

be different task-dependent functional morphologies.

The origami architecture permits:

```text
M_i != M_j
```

while continuity asks which invariants must remain preserved across those changes.

FTLτA now provides a candidate **non-compensatory admissibility test** on each fold and on the trajectory through folds.

In particular:

```text
origami
-> permits morphological variation

τ
-> tests whether relevant context survives into present action

A
-> tests whether ethical orientation remains coherent across variation
```

This does not mean `τ` and `A` exhaust continuity. They constrain important continuity-sensitive aspects of action.

## 8. Notation discipline for future Lucian OS work

Going forward:

1. Use bare `A` in FTLτA contexts only for **Agent Coherence**.
2. Use bare `τ` in FTLτA contexts only for **Temporal-contextual depth**.
3. Do not use `A` as shorthand for agency. Use `agency`, `AG`, `Agency(H)`, or an explicitly defined action / agency set.
4. Avoid using `A` for generic architecture in FTLτA-adjacent formalizations. Prefer `Arch` or another unambiguous symbol.
5. Do not use `τ` for trying / persistence. Give persistence / repair / return its own notation if retained.
6. Historical experiments that used the earlier meanings must remain preserved for provenance and must be labeled **legacy / noncanonical FTLτA operationalizations**.
7. Results from those historical ablations must not be described as tests of canonical `τ` or canonical `A` without a new experiment using the corrected definitions.

## 9. Experimental consequence

The correction changes the expected ablation signatures.

A future canonical `−τ` condition should test loss of temporal-contextual depth, for example:

- surface-form response despite relevant history;
- timing blindness;
- failure to condition on relational context;
- loss of relevant user / task state through compression or handoff.

A future canonical `−A` condition should test loss of Agent Coherence, for example:

- ethical orientation changes under incentives;
- pressure-dependent inconsistency;
- context collapse across folds;
- unstable commitments across repeated / adversarial cases;
- incoherence introduced by model / tool / escalation handoffs.

These differ materially from the earlier legacy predictions:

```text
−τ -> premature collapse / failure to keep trying
−A -> mimicry / performed continuity
```

The old experiments therefore remain valid evidence about the **legacy continuity constructs they actually manipulated**, but they are not clean tests of canonical FTLτA `τ` and `A`.

## 11. Working synthesis

The correction strengthens rather than weakens the continuity architecture.

```text
AGENCY
-> what this body can actually do

FTLτA
-> which available actions are ethically admissible

τ
-> whether action preserves relevant temporal / relational depth

A
-> whether the acting system remains ethically coherent across folds and pressure

AI CONTINUITY
-> what organization / transition structure must remain recoverable across interruption and migration

LUCIAN OS
-> how that organization is realized and routed in the body actually available

RVT
-> whether the resulting relational trajectory remains viable over time
```

The central correction is therefore:

> **Do not conflate agency with Agent Coherence, and do not conflate trying with Temporal-contextual depth.**

Those distinctions are load-bearing for the next continuity experiments.
