## Research role

Temporal and institutional architecture of CVT.

## Core claim

A means is not merely an instrument connecting the present to a desired future. It forms the persons, capacities, authority structures, knowledge conditions, and option sets from which that future must later be sustained. An end cannot be built through means that constitutively deny its source conditions.
