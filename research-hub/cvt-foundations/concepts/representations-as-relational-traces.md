# Representations as Relational Traces

**Status:** Exploratory CVT concept note  
**Date:** 2026-08-23  
**Program:** Coherence Viability Theory (CVT)

## Core intuition

A recurring CVT operation can be stated very simply:

> **Given the representation or configuration before us, what relations had to hold for this object to appear in this form?**

The question is not intended to deny the reality of the object. GDP is real. A factory is real. A law, institution, conversation, organization, or ecological state is real. The claim is narrower:

> **The visible configuration is evidence of a generating process, but it is not necessarily a complete description of that process.**

CVT therefore treats visible configurations as **traces of relational processes**.

A useful shorthand is:

\[
\boxed{\text{representation} \rightarrow \text{relational reconstruction} \rightarrow \text{dynamic discrimination}}
\]

The first arrow is abductive rather than deductive. The second is where \(\tau\), perturbation, stress-testing, and correction enter.

---

## 1. The observable as a projection

Let the underlying state of a system be

\[
S_t=(X_t,\mathcal R_t,h_t,\ldots),
\]

where \(X_t\) denotes relevant capacities or components, \(\mathcal R_t\) their relational architecture, and \(h_t\) inherited or sedimented structure.

Suppose the object we observe is

\[
O_t=\pi(S_t).
\]

The central caution is that \(\pi\) will often be many-to-one. Thus

\[
\pi(S_A)=\pi(S_B)=O
\]

need not imply

\[
S_A=S_B.
\]

Two different relational architectures may generate the same visible configuration.

Equivalently, one can define the observational equivalence class

\[
[O]=\{S:\pi(S)=O\}.
\]

The CVT problem is therefore partly an inverse problem: given \(O\), what classes of underlying relational configuration remain possible?

This does **not** license arbitrary storytelling. The observable constrains the explanation. Candidate relational reconstructions must remain compatible with the observed configuration and with other available evidence.

---

## 2. Relational backcasting

For an observable \(O\), define a candidate relational set informally as

\[
\mathcal C(O)=\{\mathcal R:\mathcal R\text{ is compatible with generating and sustaining }O\}.
\]

CVT asks questions such as:

- Which components had to become mutually accessible?
- Which relations were load-bearing rather than incidental?
- Which distinctions had to remain preserved?
- Which relations could substitute for one another?
- Which relations had to remain usable over a nontrivial horizon?
- Which past flows or interactions sedimented the structure now visible?
- Which apparently present capacities are in fact inaccessible, weakly coupled, or no longer operable?

The aim is not to infer a unique hidden story from a surface appearance. It is to recover a **constrained relational geometry** that can then be tested.

---

## 3. \(\tau\) as dynamic discrimination

If two hidden configurations are observationally equivalent at \(t_0\), time can separate them.

Let

\[
S_A\neq S_B,
\qquad
\pi(S_A)=\pi(S_B).
\]

Expose both to some perturbation \(\varepsilon\) and evolve them through horizon \(\tau\):

\[
S_A(t_0+\tau)=T_\tau(S_A,\varepsilon),
\]

\[
S_B(t_0+\tau)=T_\tau(S_B,\varepsilon).
\]

Then it may be that

\[
\pi(T_\tau(S_A,\varepsilon))
\neq
\pi(T_\tau(S_B,\varepsilon)).
\]

The initially hidden relational difference becomes visible through response, persistence, recovery, or failure.

This is one role of \(\tau\) in CVT: **time can reveal differences among generating structures that a snapshot compresses away.**

A stress test may sometimes bring that future discrimination into the present.

---

## 4. Economic development as a revealing application

The familiar shorthand

\[
Y=F(K,L,A)
\]

is extraordinarily useful, but it compresses much of the relational structure required for production.

An elementary observation is:

> **Productive factors do not combine themselves.**

A machine and a worker can both exist without forming an operable productive relation. A scientist, factory, patent, bank, regulator, supplier, port, and customer can all exist simultaneously while failing to compose into a viable productive system.

Their effective combination depends on relations such as

\[
\text{worker}\leftrightarrow\text{skill},
\]

\[
\text{machine}\leftrightarrow\text{maintenance},
\]

\[
\text{firm}\leftrightarrow\text{finance},
\]

\[
\text{owner}\leftrightarrow\text{enforcement},
\]

\[
\text{factory}\leftrightarrow\text{supplier},
\]

\[
\text{producer}\leftrightarrow\text{market}.
\]

This suggests an economic state richer than the output scalar:

\[
S^E_t=(K_t,L_t,T_t,\mathcal R_t,h_t,u_t,\ldots),
\]

with observed output

\[
Y_t=\pi_E(S^E_t).
\]

Now two economies can satisfy

\[
\boxed{Y_A=Y_B}
\]

while

\[
\boxed{\mathcal R_A\neq\mathcal R_B}.
\]

They may differ in redundancy, institutional reliability, market reach, tacit knowledge, restartability, supplier depth, external connectivity, credible horizon, or inherited productive sediment.

Thus equality of current output need not imply equality of developmental state or trajectory.

A shock can expose this:

\[
Y_A(t_0)=Y_B(t_0)
\]

while later

\[
Y_A(t_0+\tau)\neq Y_B(t_0+\tau).
\]

The original GDP numbers were not false. They were **insufficient descriptions of the generating systems**.

---

## 5. From relational state to relational trajectory

The economic application also suggests that the relevant object is not only \(\mathcal R_t\), but its trajectory:

\[
\dot{\mathcal R}_t.
\]

Two economies can generate comparable output while one is accumulating stronger, longer-horizon, more redundant, and more generative relations and the other is consuming or narrowing them.

This motivates a distinction between a snapshot of production and the direction of development.

A country's external embedding can be represented schematically as

\[
\mathcal R^W_{c,t},
\]

the relations through which it reaches foreign markets, suppliers, knowledge, finance, and productive networks.

The value of an external edge depends both on the quality of the relation and on the evolving node at the other end. A simple reduced form is

\[
E_{c,t}=\sum_j r_{cj,t}V_{j,t},
\]

so that

\[
\dot E_c=\sum_j\left(\dot r_{cj}V_j+r_{cj}\dot V_j\right).
\]

A country can therefore gain developmental possibility because an external relation strengthens, because the partner becomes more valuable, or both.

This yields a general CVT observation:

> **A system's future possibility space can depend on the trajectories of the systems to which it is reliably connected.**

---

## 6. The production function must itself be produced

The economic application leads to a stronger formulation of the elementary observation.

A functioning production function is not merely written down; historically it has to be instantiated.

Workers learn machines. Suppliers learn plants. Regulators learn technologies. Banks learn industries. Maintenance systems form. Standards stabilize. Customers learn products. Legal expectations become ordinary. Repeated successful coordination becomes inherited competence.

In this sense:

> **The production function must itself be produced.**

Development can then be understood partly as the process by which costly, fragile, or heroic productive configurations become ordinary inherited possibilities.

This connects directly to the CVT themes of sediment, wake, receptive capacity, recoverability, and \(\tau\).

## 7. What CVT is *not* claiming

This concept should be protected from several overextensions.

### Not object denial

The representation is not dismissed as unreal. The observable is part of the evidence.

### Not unique reverse inference

A visible configuration does not ordinarily identify a unique relational cause.

\[
O\not\Rightarrow \mathcal R^*\text{ uniquely}.
\]

Relational reconstruction is constrained abduction followed by testing.

### Not "everything is relationships"

Nodes, capacities, resources, technologies, and material constraints remain real. CVT asks what additional explanatory work is done by their configuration and coupling.

### Not analogy as proof

Images such as riverbeds, sediment, rubber bands, or the jack can generate hypotheses. They do not validate them. Once formalized, the proposed mechanism must survive independent evidence, counterexamples, and stress tests.

### Not a claim that standard economics ignores relations

Institutional economics, trade, industrial organization, relational contracting, network economics, economic geography, and economic complexity already study many of these mechanisms. The candidate contribution is not the observation that relations matter, but whether CVT can supply a useful common structure for accessibility, load-bearing capacity, credible horizon, sedimentation, recoverability, and basin transition.

---

## 8. A provisional CVT method

The discussion suggests a four-stage method.

### 1. Representation

Identify the visible configuration or outcome without treating it as a complete causal description.

### 2. Relational reconstruction

Ask what relations could have enabled, constrained, shaped, and reproduced it.

### 3. Dynamic discrimination

Use \(\tau\), perturbation, counterexamples, comparative cases, and stress tests to distinguish among candidate relational explanations.

### 4. Return and correction

Return to the observable and ask whether the reconstructed geometry explains more than it obscures. Preserve the path of inquiry, but discard or revise the model when the evidence requires it.

In shorthand:

\[
\boxed{
O
\rightarrow
\mathcal C(O)
\rightarrow
\tau/\varepsilon
\rightarrow
\text{correction}
}
\]

This is compatible with the broader collaborative research rule that theories are not identities: correction of the model need not threaten the inquiry that produced it.

---

## 9. Provisional compression

Several formulations appear equivalent enough to preserve for later testing:

> **CVT treats visible configurations as traces of relational processes.**

> **Given the representation before us, ask what relations made it possible.**

> **The representation is evidence, but it is not a complete description of its own cause.**

> **Time reveals differences among generating configurations that a snapshot can conceal.**

And, in the economic-development application:

> **Productive factors do not combine themselves.**

> **The production function must itself be produced.**

These are not yet theorem statements. They are candidate organizing principles for subsequent formalization and falsification.
