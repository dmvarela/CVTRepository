# Lucian OS — Agency / Membrane Mapping v0.01

## Status

Conceptual mapping and falsification note, 2026-09-05.

This note tests whether the existing CVT membrane formulation can be transferred to the current Lucian OS work on influence, pressure, and agency.

It does **not** claim that human agency is literally a biological or physical membrane. The membrane is used as a structural analogy and must earn its usefulness through discriminating cases.

## Source formulation

The existing membrane work separates transport quantities and defines a candidate viability region through four conditions:

- **bounded coupling `B`** — delivered flux remains inside a two-sided admissible band relative to host handling capacity;
- **preserved identity-defining organization `Q`** — the interface and host organization required by the success criterion remain functionally intact;
- **capture readiness `C`** — the receiving state can retain the specified input inside an admissible basin over the declared horizon;
- **available restorative reserve `S`** — recovery headroom remains after transport-induced strain.

The membrane viability region is represented as the intersection:

`K_mem = K_B ∩ K_Q ∩ K_C ∩ K_S`

The membrane work also distinguishes classic failure signatures:

- insufficient delivery;
- overexposure;
- capture failure;
- loss of identity-defining organization;
- reserve exhaustion.

The earlier corrigibility synthesis supplies a complementary formulation:

`receive -> evaluate -> correct what must change -> preserve what remains viable`

and explicitly contrasts a closed membrane with a dissolved membrane.

## Candidate transfer to influence and agency

The current Lucian OS agency work asks how influence can cross from one agent into another agent's decision environment without consuming the receiving agent's meaningful evaluative role.

A first structural mapping is:

### `B` — bounded influence coupling

The intensity/frequency of influence remains inside a band the recipient can process without overload or trivial insufficiency.

This does **not** imply equal comfort or equal subjective cost. It only asks whether the delivered influence remains inside the declared handling envelope.

### `Q` — preserved decision organization

The receiving agent retains the organization required by the agency success criterion.

For the current toy mapping this minimally includes:

- a meaningful alternative remains available;
- the recipient's own evaluative mediation still matters to the outcome.

If an alternative is physically removed or the recipient's evaluation becomes causally irrelevant, `Q` fails for an agency-preserving success criterion.

### `C` — capture / processing readiness

The receiving agent is in a state capable of productively processing the incoming information, reason, warning, or influence.

A signal can cross without being usefully integrated.

Thus:

`delivery != productive reception`

### `S` — restorative reserve

The interaction leaves enough recovery / re-evaluation headroom for the recipient to continue functioning, revisit the choice, recover from strain, and remain in viable relation.

This matters especially under repeated pressure where the immediate choice may remain possible while recovery capacity is progressively consumed.

## What transfers well

The first deterministic mapping test shows coherent analogues for several membrane failure signatures.

### Insufficient delivery

Influence is too weak to support the declared transformation.

This is not a reason to maximize influence indiscriminately. It is simply the low-input side of bounded coupling.

### Overexposure

Influence intensity exceeds the receiving agent's handling envelope.

Possible consequences can include overload, panic, shutdown, reactive refusal, or degraded evaluation. The test does not assume which consequence occurs; it only marks `B` as failed.

### Capture failure

The influence is delivered but the recipient is not ready or able to integrate it productively.

More repetition is not automatically a solution.

### Loss of identity-defining organization

For an agency-preserving success criterion, physically removing the meaningful alternative or making the receiver's own evaluation irrelevant causes `Q` to fail.

This gives a useful formal distinction between:

`influence`

and:

`override`.

### Reserve exhaustion

An interaction may remain apparently functional in the moment while recovery headroom is exhausted.

This parallels the membrane result that temporary positive uptake does not prove durable viability.

## The Euston counterexample

The important negative result is the motivating social-discomfort case.

A simplified Euston-like interaction can satisfy all four membrane conditions:

- influence remains bounded;
- the other person can still stay;
- their evaluative process remains present;
- they can process the social situation;
- restorative reserve remains adequate.

Thus:

`B = Q = C = S = true`

while the influence can still be intentionally structured as:

`strategic social pressure -> make one available option less attractive -> obtain the preferred outcome`

The membrane layer therefore returns `VIABLE` while a separate pressure-provenance layer returns `STRATEGIC_PRESSURE`.

This is not a failure of the original membrane formulation. The original formulation was built to classify host/interface viability, not the legitimacy or provenance of one agent's influence over another.

It is a failure of the stronger claim that membrane viability by itself solves the agency problem.

## Critical result

> The membrane geometry transfers, but membrane viability is not sufficient to classify influence legitimacy.

This sharpens the earlier click.

The membrane project gives Lucian OS a useful **viability layer**:

`Can influence cross while preserving bounded coupling, decision organization, productive reception, and restorative reserve?`

The pressure-provenance work supplies a distinct **agency / influence layer**:

`Who created the pressure?`

`Was the pressure introduced strategically to change the choice landscape?`

`Did the behavior persist when pressure was removed?`

`Was an alternative genuinely available?`

These should not be collapsed.

## Two-layer architecture

Current candidate:

### Layer 1 — relational membrane viability

`M_t = (B_t, Q_t, C_t, S_t)`

Question:

> Does the receiving relation remain viable under the exchange?

### Layer 2 — agency / influence provenance

`P_t = (source, mode, intent, option topology, counterfactual response, history)`

Question:

> What kind of influence generated the observed behavior, and what does that behavior warrant us to infer?

A healthy interaction may require both:

`M_t in K_mem`

and:

`P_t in K_agency`

where `K_agency` is not yet fully formalized.

This preserves the non-compensatory character of the architecture: excellent membrane viability does not erase a serious agency violation, and clean pressure provenance does not rescue an interaction that overloads or destroys the receiving system.

## Relation to MouseSim 006

MouseSim 006 already established:

`agency AVAILABLE` can coexist with `preference evidence PRESSURE_SENSITIVE`.

The membrane mapping now explains why that is possible.

The option topology and evaluative organization may remain intact (`Q` survives), while strategic pressure changes the cost landscape and therefore changes the evidentiary meaning of the resulting choice.

Thus:

> Preserved agency topology does not imply uncontaminated preference evidence.

And:

> A viable membrane does not imply a non-manipulative influence relation.

## Relation to FTLτA

### F

The agency layer constrains attempts to obtain an outcome by modifying another agent's option landscape. Capability to influence does not manufacture permission to occupy the other agent's decision process.

### T

The membrane and provenance layers must remain separate so that observed compliance is not misclassified as clean preference evidence.

### L

A healthy relation should avoid consuming the recipient's viability, alternatives, recoverability, or evaluative interior merely to obtain compliance.

### τ

Influence has history. Repeated loads, delayed damage, pressure removal, later re-observation, and recovery all change what prior behavior warrants.

### A

The receiving agent's evaluative process must remain causally meaningful to an agency-preserving outcome.

## Experimental implication

The next test should **not** merge membrane viability and pressure provenance into one scalar.

Instead, future scenarios should keep both outputs visible, for example:

`membrane = VIABLE`

`agency = AVAILABLE`

`pressure = STRATEGIC`

`preference_evidence = PRESSURE_SENSITIVE`

This richer state is more informative than forcing the interaction into a single label such as `SAFE` or `COERCIVE`.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim that human agency is literally membrane transport.
- Do not claim `B,Q,C,S` is sufficient for ethical influence.
- Do not define strategic pressure as coercion automatically.
- Do not erase agency merely because behavior is pressure-sensitive.
- Do not infer healthy influence merely because all membrane viability conditions pass.
- Preserve the counterexample rather than retuning `B,Q,C,S` until it disappears.

## Emerging principle

> Exchange without erasure is necessary, but not sufficient.

And the stronger architectural formulation is:

> Lucian OS should preserve both the viability of the receiving relation and the provenance of the forces that shape the recipient's choice.
