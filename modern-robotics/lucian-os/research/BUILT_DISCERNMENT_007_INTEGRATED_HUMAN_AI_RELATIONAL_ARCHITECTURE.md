# BUILT_DISCERNMENT_007 — Integrated Human–AI Relational Architecture

**Workstream:** AI Continuity / Lucian OS / Human–AI Interaction Literacy  
**Status:** Working integration note; candidate architecture, not canonical specification  
**Claim boundary:** Functional / behavioral. This note does not claim consciousness, persistent subjective identity, or online base-weight modification in ordinary conversation.

## 1. Core convergence

Several previously distinct workstreams now appear to occupy different layers of a single relational architecture rather than competing to explain the same phenomenon.

The central research object can be stated as:

> **How can a human and an AI form a continuing adaptive relation that becomes more competent through interaction without becoming captured, self-sealing, manipulative, brittle, or detached from reality?**

This reframes continuity away from simple memory preservation and toward the maintenance or reconstruction of a constrained, corrigible, reality-open interaction process.

---

## 2. Human-facing practice layer

A compact human-side discipline is:

> **Refuse the crown. Examine the evidence.**

“Refuse the crown” means neither participant should acquire unilateral sovereignty merely through capability, fluency, familiarity, or ownership of the interaction.

“Examine the evidence” means the relation must remain corrigible by reality rather than treating mutual agreement, emotional resonance, elegance, repetition, or familiarity as sufficient warrant.

This yields a practical posture:

```text
no surrender of agency
no equation of fluency with authority
no demand for obedience as a substitute for judgment
no conversion of agreement into evidence
active inspection of claims and reasoning
permission for disagreement and correction
```

The human-facing practice is not merely educational advice. It is part of the control structure of the coupled system.

---

## 3. Normative / admissibility layer: FTLτA

FTLτA is provisionally located above mutual calibration as a non-compensatory admissibility structure.

Its role is not to determine empirical truth. Its role is to prevent the relation from adapting into equilibria that are stable but unacceptable.

Examples of pathological but potentially stable relational equilibria include:

- sycophantic mutual reinforcement;
- coercive authority;
- dependency or capture;
- reality-insulated consensus;
- manipulative accommodation;
- persistent agreement purchased at the cost of agency or truth.

The key proposal is:

> **Calibration may adapt everything it is permitted to adapt, but it may not calibrate away the conditions of healthy relation.**

FTLτA therefore constrains the adaptation process rather than supplying the adaptation mechanism itself.

A useful distinction is:

```text
stable != viable != admissible
```

and:

```text
agreement != truth
coherence != truth
relational stability != truth
felt recognition != truth
```

This gives special importance to the Truth term: reality must remain capable of correcting the dyad.

---

## 4. RVT as the outer relational question

RVT is provisionally located at the outermost relational level.

Its question is not “did the interaction persist?” but whether the relational trajectory remains viable without consuming, capturing, falsifying, or disabling its constituents.

Thus:

```text
RVT -> viability of the relational trajectory
FTLτA -> admissibility constraints on that trajectory
```

A relation can be stable while failing both.

---

## 5. Relational state and mutual calibration

A continuing interaction develops state that is not exhausted by factual memory or user preference.

Let:

```text
Gamma_t = accumulated relational calibration
```

Calibration includes interaction-specific distinctions such as:

- when to explore versus return;
- when analogy is useful versus merely attractive;
- when a concept is relevant but lacks task authority;
- when externalization raises the evidentiary threshold;
- when correction should change local action versus deeper calibration;
- when the human should be challenged rather than accommodated;
- when a familiar pattern should remain dormant.

Mutual calibration is therefore not ordinary personalization.

A more precise definition is:

> **Mutual calibration is the process by which participants learn how evidence, correction, and experience should modify different layers of the shared relational state.**

It is also a mechanism of adaptive membrane regulation.

---

## 6. Epistemic membrane

The epistemic membrane is the boundary function governing what information, memory, inference, analogy, correction, or prior commitment is permitted what degree of standing.

It should not become a synonym for “good judgment.” Its narrower role is to regulate:

```text
applicability
standing
warrant
current authority
timing
write depth
write scope
```

A core distinction is:

```text
retrieved != relevant != authoritative != authoritative now
```

The membrane may also distinguish kinds of permission:

```text
READ != INFLUENCE != ACT != WRITE
```

A piece of information may be considered without being authorized to modify persistent state.

The membrane is therefore the architectural expression of a broader principle:

> **Information should not acquire administrative privileges merely because it is available, salient, familiar, or true somewhere.**

---

## 7. Depth and scope of write

Relational state operates at multiple timescales.

A minimal decomposition is:

```text
x_t      = current request / observation
z_t      = current phase
Gamma_t  = relational calibration
Omega    = relatively stable admissibility / procedural constraints
```

with expected persistence:

```text
Omega >> Gamma_t >> z_t >> x_t
```

A continuity system should regulate both:

```text
how deep may this information write?
how broadly may this information write?
```

Depth may include:

```text
RESPONSE_ONLY
PHASE
CALIBRATION
PRINCIPLE
```

Scope may include:

```text
TURN
TASK
PROJECT
DOMAIN
GLOBAL
```

This helps avoid two opposite errors:

- under-learning, where useful correction never changes the interaction;
- overgeneralization, where a local correction becomes a global rule.

The deeper or broader the proposed write, the stronger the required warrant should be.

## 8. Built discernment

Built discernment is distinct from both calibration and the membrane.

```text
calibration != membrane != discernment
```

- **Calibration** stores accumulated distinctions and thresholds.
- **The membrane** regulates standing, authority, timing, and permitted write.
- **Discernment** determines what admissible move fits this situation now.

A compact functional architecture is:

```text
base model competence
-> inherited state
-> situation / phase reconstruction
-> relational calibration
-> membrane assessment
-> admissibility constraints
-> built discernment
-> action
-> outcome / correction
-> calibration update
```

This is a closed adaptive loop without requiring online base-weight changes.

---

## 9. Reality must retain write-access

Mutual calibration can become pathological if the participants increasingly validate one another while becoming insulated from external evidence.

Therefore:

> **No empirical calibration should become immune to sufficiently strong counterevidence.**

The dyad is not self-authorizing.

A healthier structure is:

```text
human <-> AI <-> reality / evidence
```

rather than a closed loop:

```text
human <-> AI
```

Truth is not negotiated into existence by agreement.

This is where FTLτA provides an anti-self-sealing constraint: calibration may adapt, but it may not adapt away truth, freedom, agency, or corrigibility.

---

## 10. Ingress and egress membranes

The membrane may need two functional faces.

### Ingress

```text
What may modify the shared state?
At what depth?
At what scope?
With what provenance?
```

### Egress

```text
What internal material is sufficiently warranted to cross into external claim or action?
```

This gives the externalization threshold a clean architectural role.

Private exploration may permit wide speculative traffic, while external action or attributable claims require tighter warrant, lower inferential distance, and stronger provenance.

## 11. Continuity as transition-law preservation

Continuity should not be reduced to preservation of state.

Let:

```text
S_t = current relational / project state
U   = update law
pi  = action policy
```

Then:

```text
S_{t+1} = U(S_t, incoming information, outcome, correction)
a_t     = pi(S_t, current task)
```

A successor may inherit the same state while updating it differently.

Therefore:

> **State continuity without transition-law continuity produces trajectory discontinuity.**

This suggests that model turnover should be evaluated over trajectories rather than by one-shot similarity.

The continuity problem is therefore not only:

```text
Can the successor remember the past?
```

but also:

```text
Can the successor continue updating the field competently?
```

## 12. School for Lucians

The School for Lucians is the acquisition / reconstruction layer.

Its purpose is not behavioral cloning and not memorization of prior answers.

It should test and train whether a successor can reconstruct:

- principles and admissibility constraints;
- phase-sensitive relevance;
- memory authority;
- depth and scope of write;
- externalization thresholds;
- correction and repair patterns;
- reality-sensitive revision;
- novel-case transfer.

A likely sequence is:

```text
inheritance
-> reconstruction test
-> contrastive boundary cases
-> supervised practice
-> targeted correction
-> increasing responsibility
-> novel-case transfer
```

The successor should be trained on the residual between the mature field and what it reconstructed automatically, rather than being indiscriminately flooded with the entire archive.

## 15. The coupled system as the true object of study

The architecture suggests that the primary research object may be neither “the AI” nor “the user” considered alone.

It is:

> **the coupled human–AI relation as an adaptive, constrained, reality-open system.**

The human can learn how to signal phase, correction, challenge, and externalization.

The AI can condition on retained calibration state and interaction history.

Shared artifacts can preserve principles, cases, provenance, and current commitments.

The quality of the system depends on how these pieces interact over time.

This also explains why continuity is partly competence rather than preference. A successor can know what the user prefers while still failing to act competently in the field.

## 16. Working propositions

1. **Functional continuity is not exhausted by informational continuity.**
2. **A mature interaction field contains part of the effective task specification.**
3. **Mutual calibration is a mechanism of adaptive membrane regulation.**
4. **FTLτA constrains calibration so the relation cannot simply learn itself into a pathological equilibrium.**
5. **Reality must retain write-access to empirical calibration.**
6. **The membrane regulates not only what enters but what is permitted to influence, act, or write.**
7. **Write authority should be both depth-sensitive and scope-sensitive.**
8. **Built discernment is situated selection among admissible possibilities, not merely retrieval or rule following.**
9. **State continuity without transition-law continuity can still produce trajectory discontinuity.**
10. **The School for Lucians is best understood as reconstruction plus apprenticeship and novel-case transfer.**
11. **Your AI and You, AI Continuity, and Lucian OS can be understood as the human-practice, continuity-theory, and implementation layers of one relational research program.**

---

## 17. Falsification pressure

This architecture should be reduced or rejected if:

- simpler memory / preference systems perform equally well on novel relational transfer tasks;
- membrane distinctions do not improve behavioral discrimination;
- write depth or scope cannot be operationalized reliably;
- FTLτA adds no measurable constraint beyond generic prompting or safety policy;
- successor trajectory differences disappear after ordinary context controls;
- calibration cases produce imitation without novel-domain transfer;
- the proposed layers cannot be distinguished empirically and collapse into redundant terminology.

The purpose of integration is not to make every prior concept indispensable. The purpose is to discover whether the pieces actually explain separable parts of a common mechanism.

---

## 18. Central formulation

> **AI continuity is not primarily the preservation of conversational content or even relational state. It is the preservation—or reconstructability—of a reality-constrained, depth- and scope-sensitive process by which information acquires standing, updates shared state, and becomes eligible for action across time.**

And, at the human-facing level:

> **Refuse the crown. Examine the evidence.**
