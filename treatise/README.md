# Treatise Workspace

This directory is the architectural workspace for the larger relational-viability treatise emerging from the CVT research program.

The purpose of this workspace is **not** to replace the individual papers. Papers remain the appropriate container for bounded theorems, proofs, empirical tests, and journal arguments. The treatise records the larger architecture that those papers increasingly appear to be cross-sections of.

## Working premise

The recurring object across the research program is the formation, viability, reproduction, robustness, and adaptation of relations.

A provisional spine is:

1. **Relations and viability**
2. **Formation and coupled activation**
3. **Capability accumulation**
4. **Graduation**
5. **Generativity**
6. **Accretion**
7. **Reproductive closure**
8. **Robustness**
9. **Adaptive embeddedness**
10. **Applications**

The architecture is defeasible. It is a map of the current object, not a commitment to preserve every label or module.

## Treatise / paper division of labor

- **Treatise:** carries the full conceptual architecture, cross-paper dependencies, confidence layers, open questions, and connections among applications.
- **Papers:** isolate claims mature enough for proof, formal analysis, empirical identification, or journal-level argument.
- **Notes:** preserve excavations, analogies, counterexamples, adversarial reviews, and candidate structures that may later be promoted or discarded.

## Epistemic discipline

Every substantive element should be classifiable as one of:

- proved result;
- inherited mathematics;
- formal conjecture;
- proposed mechanism;
- empirical hypothesis;
- cross-domain analogy;
- open question.

The treatise should never blur these categories merely to produce a cleaner narrative.

## Current center of gravity

The development-economics strand has recently exposed a gated propagation structure:

`possibility -> activation -> capability accumulation -> graduation -> generativity -> reproductive closure -> robustness -> adaptive embeddedness`

This sequence currently provides the clearest bridge between the formation dynamics being developed in Paper F and the closure/robustness results developed in Paper E.

A trajectory note rotates the development object from a level/state representation toward an ongoing process of propagation, self-maintenance, supersession, and adaptation in a changing capability landscape. It introduces healing-front mathematics only as a candidate propagation model and distinguishes development from convergence by comparing domestic developmental-front speed with a moving external frontier.

The adaptive-memory note asks what happens when productive trajectories alter the network they traverse. After hostile review, its scope is now explicitly narrower: `(Q_t,W_t)` is a candidate **productive-memory/pathway-formation module**, not a complete theory of development. The revised note separates exploration, successful reinforcement, failed-transaction damage, and decay; treats bilateral complementarity as a possible source of threshold dynamics rather than assuming bistability from `Q <-> W`; and requires scarce/feasible exploration rather than universal edge seeding.

The earlier smooth generativity construction based on `H_ij = partial gamma_j / partial x_i`, `H^k`, and `K=RH` has been withdrawn because Paper F's activation variable is binary. The current bridge is discrete:

`Paper F graduation -> generative graduation cascade -> Paper E reproductive closure`.

The discrete cascade note formalizes a graduated set `A`, a Paper-F-style graduation gate `g_e(A)`, the inflationary operator `Phi(A)`, finite generative graduation closure `C_G(A_0)`, sequential cascade layers `L_k`, cascade depth, seed generativity, joint-seed/nucleation problems, resource-constrained realizations, and the explicit distinction `graduation != propagation != reproductive closure`. It treats the bridge as a positive-complementarity benchmark and records where signed interactions, scarce coordination, changing environments, and the still-open economic map into Paper E can break the simple closure logic.

A dedicated hostile-review record preserves the failed constructions, the referee objections, and the current adjudication so discarded claims are not quietly reintroduced later.
